Game concept art in the style of a 1955 industrial-bureaucracy conglomerate. Materials: riveted steel panels, brushed gunmetal, manila folders, kraft paper, rubber stamps, forms in triplicate with carbon paper, bakelite knobs and switches, anachronistic CRT terminals with warm amber phosphor screens. Palette: warm paper tones (manila, cream, kraft) and steel grays; the ONLY saturated accents are stamp red and approval green. Lighting: warm tungsten desk lamps against cool industrial shadow. Mood: dry institutional order; bureaucratic, deadpan, meticulous, quietly charming — like a beloved mid-century government facility run with absolute sincerity. Any visible signage, letterheads or department names belong to the fictional conglomerate "WERKZ" — never render any real-world brand, company or show name. Wherever the WERKZ logomark appears, it is a bold geometric letter W like stamped steel, wide and grounded, chevron-like, with its center vertex distinctly LOWER than the two outer peaks (canonical letterform: anchors/werkz-logo-hero-letterform.png). Rendering: clean painterly shapes with crisp silhouettes, readable at mobile-game scale. Strictly avoid: neon colors, cyberpunk, green-on-black hacker aesthetics, glitch effects, clownish cartoon exaggeration.
