Mobile game UI mockup in portrait phone aspect: a physical paper requisition document sliding up from the bottom of the screen over a dimmed workshop scene. The document has a typewriter header reading "REQUISITION 47-B", perforated edges, carbon-copy texture, and a central panel showing a stylized abstract code diff — blocks of green added lines and red removed lines, abstract marks only, no real readable code or filenames. Interaction affordances: swipe-left hint toward a "DENIED" rubber stamp in stamp red, swipe-right hint toward an "APPROVED" rubber stamp in approval green, both rendered as chunky physical stamps with ink texture. Tactile, weighty, satisfying — a form you want to slam a stamp onto.
